## What the confidence ratings mean

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `high` | Published contract or salary figure that is documented in multiple places and not seriously disputed. |
| `medium` | Sourced to a salary database (Baseball-Reference, Basketball-Reference, Spotrac, the MLS Players Association) or derived from a well-documented contract total. Reliable enough to play on, worth spot-checking. |
| `low` | Press estimate, reconstructed from a contract total, converted from a foreign currency, or otherwise contested. **Every entry below needs a real source before launch.** |

`low` entry from the daily selection, so the game is playable on checked data
while the rest is being worked through.

## Known systemic issues

1. **`leagueAvgThatSeason` is approximate throughout.** These are league-wide means assembled from union figures and press summaries, rounded, and in some cases interpolated between published years. They are surfaced in the UI as "approx." and drive the in-game hint, so a wrong one makes a puzzle unfairly hard rather than merely inaccurate. 19 entries have no figure at all (`null`).

2. **Soccer wages are conversions, not salaries.** Every European entry started as a weekly figure in GBP or EUR, was annualised, and was converted at an approximate period exchange rate. Several are net-of-tax figures grossed up by estimate. None is rated above `medium`, and the MLS/NWSL entries — where the players' union publishes actual contracts — are the only soccer figures worth trusting.

3. **Pre-1980 figures across all sports are press estimates.** Before free agency and players' unions, salaries were private and reported second-hand. Where two accounts disagree, the more widely repeated figure is used and the entry is rated down.

4. **NFL "salary" is ambiguous by construction.** Base salary, cash earned, and cap hit can differ by an order of magnitude in the same season. This file uses cash earned where it is known and base salary otherwise, and says which in `source`. Rookie-contract entries look tiny because the signing bonus was paid in a different year — that is real, not an error.

5. **Contract AAV is not a season salary.** Entries derived from a multi-year total (Reggie Jackson 1977, Catfish Hunter 1975, Pete Maravich 1970, and others) use the annual average value. The actual season-by-season payments were usually uneven.

- Any player whose salary could only be sourced to a listicle or an aggregator with no primary reference.
